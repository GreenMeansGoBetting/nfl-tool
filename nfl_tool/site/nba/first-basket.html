The Matchup

Road team on the left, home team on the right, what the number is in the middle with the league average under it. Each number is colored against the league from its own team's side: green = good for that team (for First FG Allowed, red = that position scores first against them often). The Edge pill points at the better side when they're clearly apart. The Tip: the jumper is his team's most frequent jumper among tonight's starters; road / home rows use tonight's spot. Scoring First: any first points count, free throws included. Opening Trips: possessions and seconds to a team's first points, and what its defense lets opponents do (more = tougher D). Wins the tip and Scores first are the fair guess: the two jumpers (steadied toward 50%) and how often the tip winner scores first league-wide.

Starters

The starting five from the NBA's lineup file: Expected until the team turns its five in, then Confirmed (about 30 minutes before tip); the page checks every minute. Before the NBA posts one, Our guess: the five who started most when they played, leaving out anyone listed Out. The jumper, the tip and the fair guess follow whichever five is shown. Every number is from his starts; new = no starts on file in this season's view. Fair is a rough guess at his price to score first, not a pick: his team's chance to score first, split by each starter's first scores per start (steadied toward his teammates'). It doesn't know about matchups; that part is yours. Shot early: starts where he took a shot before anyone scored. 1st shot: he took the game's first shot. Scored 1st: he scored the game's first points.

Data: sportsdataverse / hoopR (ESPN play-by-play and box scores). Any first points count, free throws included.
