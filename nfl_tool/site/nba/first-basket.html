The Tip

The jumper is the player who took the most opening tips for his team, among its likely starters. Tips won counts his opening jumps that his team came away with. Team tip % is how often the team won the opening tip in that spot (home or away). The bar is the head-to-head guess from the two jumpers, each steadied toward 50% so a handful of jumps can't run away with it.

Scoring First

Any first points count, free throws included (how most books settle First Basket). After winning / losing the tip shows how much the tip matters for each team. Q1 margin is the average first-quarter score difference: do they start fast? Green = good for that team; the small line under each is the league.

Opening Trips

How many possessions, and how many seconds, a team needs for its first points, next to what the other defense allows. Each side is colored from its own point of view: green offense = scores quickly, green defense = makes teams work for their first points.

First Field Goal Allowed

Who made the first field goal against each defense, by position (ESPN lists players only as guard, forward or center). Green = that position scores first against this defense more than it does league-wide.

Starters

The starting five from the NBA's lineup file: Expected until the team turns its five in, then Confirmed (about 30 minutes before tip); the page checks every minute. Before the NBA posts one, Our guess: the five who started most when they played, leaving out anyone listed Out. The jumper, the tip and the fair guess follow whichever five is shown. Every number is from his starts; new = no starts on file in this season's view. Shot early: starts where he took a shot before anyone scored. 1st shot: he took the game's first shot. Scored 1st: he scored the game's first points.

Fair Value Guess

A rough guess, not a pick. The tip from the two jumpers; the first score from the tip and how often the tip winner scores first league-wide; each starter's share from his first scores per start, steadied toward his teammates'. It doesn't know about matchups or the opening trips above. That part is yours.

Data: sportsdataverse / hoopR (ESPN play-by-play and box scores). Any first points count, free throws included.
