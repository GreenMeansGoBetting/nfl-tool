Each number is colored against the league from its own team's side: green = good for that team (First FG Allowed: red = that position scores first against them often). The Edge pill points at the better side when they're clearly apart. Prediction: each jumper's chance to win tonight's tip (the two add to 100%). It comes from a rating built from every jump ball this season (opening tips, held balls, overtimes), counting who he jumped against, so beating a 7-footer counts for more than beating a guard; each rating starts from the player's height, which carries it until he has jumped a few times. Hover a number to see his jump balls. On last season's games this picked the tip winner 64% of the time, vs 61% for plain Tips Won. Tips Won: the jumper's opening tips won, in the season you picked. Scores First: any first points count, free throws included. Opening Trips: average possessions to a team's first points, and how many its defense makes opponents take (more = tougher D).

Before the NBA posts one, Our guess: the five who started most when they played, leaving out anyone listed Out. Every number is from his starts; New = no starts on file in this season's view. Team Share (the boxed columns) counts his own team's firsts, whatever the other team did: 1st Pts = he scored his team's first points (free throws count), so if Boston scores first and Cade Cunningham then scores Detroit's first points, Cade gets it; 1st Shot = he took his team's first field goal try, before or after the other team scored (free throws aren't a shot). That's why they run higher than 1st Shot and Scored 1st, which need the game's very first shot or points. Fair is our rough guess at his price to score first (not a pick).
