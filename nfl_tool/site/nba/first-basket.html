Starters

The starting five from the NBA's lineup file: Expected until the team turns its five in, then Confirmed (about 30 minutes before tip); the page checks every minute. Before the NBA posts one, Our guess: the five who started most when they played, leaving out anyone listed Out. Every number is from his starts; New = no starts on file in this season's view. Team Share (the boxed columns) counts his own team's firsts, whatever the other team did: 1st Pts = he scored his team's first points (free throws count), so if Boston scores first and Cade Cunningham then scores Detroit's first points, Cade gets it; 1st Shot = he took his team's first field goal try, before or after the other team scored (free throws aren't a shot). That's why they run higher than 1st Shot and Scored 1st, which need the game's very first shot or points. Scores First (on each team's banner): our chance that team scores the game's first points, from its jumper's Prediction and how often tip winners score first (66.6% last season); early offense and defense added nothing once the tip was known. Fair: our price for him to score the game's first points = his team's Scores First x his share of his team's first points (half how much he shoots, half his Team Share 1st Pts, steadied toward his teammates). Built from this season's games only, so early in the season the five sit close to even and spread out as games are played. Who scores a team's first points is close to random: on last season's games this picked the right starter about 25% of the time (20% for a blind guess). Best = the best First Basket price across your books (SmartStake; Novig's own price when no book has him), locked at tip-off; click it for every book, and click a book's price to save the play to My Plays at that price. Tags: Value = the Best price pays at least 10% more than our Fair; Top Option = most shots a start on his team this season; First Look = took his team's first shot in a quarter or more of his starts this season (5+ starts).

The Matchup

Road team on the left, home team on the right. Each number is colored against the league from its own team's side: green = good for that team (First FG Allowed: red = that position scores first against them often). The Edge pill points at the better side when they're clearly apart. Prediction: each jumper's chance to win tonight's tip (the two add to 100%). It comes from a rating built from every opening tip this season (held balls and overtime tips are left out), counting who he jumped against, so beating a 7-footer counts for more than beating a guard; each rating starts from the player's height, which carries it until he has jumped a few times. Hover a number to see his opening tips. On last season's games this picked the tip winner 63% of the time, vs 61% for plain Tips Won. Tips Won: the jumper's opening tips won, in the season you picked. Scores First: any first points count, free throws included. Opening Trips: average possessions to a team's first points, and how many its defense makes opponents take (more = tougher D).
