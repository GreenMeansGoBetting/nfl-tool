Slate Standouts

Every mismatch on the night's slate in one place, by category. Standouts point toward more; Tough Spots point toward less. Every row reads both sides. Team rows come from Matchup Keys: one team top 10 at something, the other bottom half (ranks of 30; 30th = the worst, red). Player rows come from Player Types: how players of his type (his colored letter) did against tonight's defense compared with their own averages (+3.1 = 3.1 points over their usual night), and in how many of those games they beat their average. He has to be a real scorer at it himself (12+ points a game for Points, 1.5+ threes for Threes). Stacked = a player spot and a team spot in the same game point the same way. Out and Doubtful players are left off. Numbers, not picks.
