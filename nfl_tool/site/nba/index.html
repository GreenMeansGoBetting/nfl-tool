The Matchup

Each row: the offense's number across from what the other defense allows, both directions side by side. Every cell is a rank of 30 (big) with its number (small), shaded green = good for that team. How-often rows (Pace, the Shares, Ball Movement): green offense = does it a lot, green defense = takes it away, so green against red = the offense gets its way. Edge = one side clearly points one way and the other doesn't push back (the logo = who it favors; Big Edge = a full league spread). Fits = the offense does it a lot and this defense allows it a lot; Takes Away = this defense rarely allows it. Rebounding = offensive rebound % against the defense's defensive rebound %. Vs Tonight's Types: the team that is the type (fast, rim attackers, a ball-hawk defense...) shows its rank in that trait; the other shows its points vs normal against teams like that, minus the league's, ranked among every team that has faced them (shown once it has 3+ games against them).
