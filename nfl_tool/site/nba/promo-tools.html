King of the Court

DraftKings pays out on the player with the most points + rebounds + assists (PRA) of the night. 40+ ... 60+ count his games at or above each mark (a 52 counts in 40+, 45+ and 50+). Score weighs those counts 1 to 5, so big nights count more. Matchup is the other team's Score for what it has given up, and vs Pos what it allows to his position (guard, forward or big) per minute against the league: green = good for him. Proj is tonight's PRA: his season and last 10 games, times what the other defense allows his position, times the game total. Lead % is his chance to finish the night on top, from playing the whole slate out thousands of times. Players listed Out are left out of every chance.

Defense board

What every team has given up. 40+ ... 60+: opposing players' games at or above each mark. Score weighs them 1 to 5. Best opp is the most PRA any one opponent had, on average a game: a high one with a low Score = a team that lets one star beat it; a high Score = a team giving it up top to bottom. vs G / F / C: PRA per 48 minutes allowed to guards, forwards and bigs. Red = bad defense (gives up the most). Teams not playing tonight are dimmed.
