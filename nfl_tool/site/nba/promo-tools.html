King of the Court

DraftKings pays out on the player with the most points + rebounds + assists (PRA) of the night. 40+ ... 60+ count his games at or above each mark (a 52 counts in 40+, 45+ and 50+). Opp Top is what the other team lets the best player in its games have: the most PRA any one opponent had, on average a game. vs Pos is what it allows his position (guard, forward or big) per minute against the league: green = good for him. Proj is tonight's PRA: his season average and last 10 games, times what the other defense allows his position, times the game total. This season's games only. Step back with the arrow to see a finished night: the board as it stood before the first tip against what each player had. Lead % is his chance to finish the night on top, from playing the whole slate out thousands of times. Players listed Out are left out of every chance.
