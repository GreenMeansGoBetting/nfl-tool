Game Predictions

Every game's GMG Projection, built from the League Stats Chart's season numbers: each team's offense against what tonight's defense allows (shooting from three, the paint and mid-range, turnovers, the offensive glass, free throws), times how many possessions the two paces make, plus 2 points of home court. Early in the season every number is pulled toward the league average until a team has played enough games for it to mean something. Point Breakdown shows where each team's points come from, row by row. On top, the book's spread and total beside ours. Book Odds and GMG Fair Odds sit side by side at the same line (the Over on the road team's row, the Under on the home team's): green = our price says that side is better than the book's price, red = worse. Hover a price for both chances. It doesn't know who is injured or resting tonight, and the betting market usually does, so treat a big gap as a reason to look, not a bet. A game that has tipped keeps the prediction and lines it had at tip; the YTD record grades our side of each market.
