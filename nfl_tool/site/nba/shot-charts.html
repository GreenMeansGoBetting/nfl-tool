Shot Charts

Pick a shooter and a defense. Left: his shots, colored by his make rate against the league's from the same spot (green = he makes more). Right: every shot that defense has allowed, colored by the make rate it allows against the league's (red = it allows more, green = it holds shooters below the league). Share = how much of the shooting comes from that zone. Target / Tough read both sides: one side clearly points one way (2.5 points or more from the league) and the other doesn't point the other way; zones he rarely shoots from get no call. Areas with only a few shots are pulled toward the league for the colors and calls; the numbers shown are the real counts. The filters apply to both sides (Last 10 = his last 10 games, and the defense's last 10). This season's shots only; spots are ESPN's, to the nearest foot; heaves (35+ ft) and free throws are left out.
