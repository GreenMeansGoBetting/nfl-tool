League Stats Chart

Every team's numbers this season, ranked across all 30 and shaded on the 8-step scale: green = 1st, red = 30th. Offense = what the team does with the ball; Defense (Allowed) = what its opponents do against it. Green is always good for that team: on offense the most of a thing is green (fastest pace, most threes); on defense allowing the least is green (and forcing the most turnovers). Hover a cell for the exact number and its rank. Show Rank swaps the numbers for ranks. Click a column to sort best first, again for worst first. Click a game above to pull its two teams to the top; click it again to put them back. Click a logo for that team's game in Matchup Breakdown. Matchup Keys = what stands out in the picked game, each key as the chart's own tiles: an Edge (one side in the top 10, the other in the bottom half), Fits / Takes Away (an offense that does something a lot against a defense that allows it the most / the least), Same Style and Clash (both teams at the same end of a number, or opposite ends), Trend (their over / under records together), Few Falling (lots of a shot, its makes 10+ places lower) and Not Tested (a weak spot tonight's offense rarely goes at). Last 10 / 5 / 3 use every game a team has until it has played that many. Shares, rates and FG% are percents; Trans Pts = points a 100 transition possessions; Ball Move = assisted makes and catch-and-shoot shots, averaged.
