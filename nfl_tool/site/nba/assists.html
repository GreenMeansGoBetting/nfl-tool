Every assists line tonight with the numbers behind it. No projections and no picks: real games from this season, so you can dig in and make the call.

What's here: his assists a game, how often he's gone over tonight's line, what tonight's defense allows his position, and how he's passed in the games a teammate on tonight's injury report missed. Line is the middle of the books' lines; Over / Under are the best prices across your books (click one for every book, the box saves it to My Plays). Hit Rate counts his games over tonight's line. Opp Allows is what tonight's opponent gives up a game to his position this season (1st = the most). Teammates On The Report shows his number in the games each teammate on tonight's injury report missed, with the change from his games with that teammate: "No" = Out or Doubtful, "If No" = Day-To-Day or Questionable, and fewer than 3 games is too few to lean on. NBA lineups change late: rest days and late scratches happen right up to tip, so check the news before you bet.
