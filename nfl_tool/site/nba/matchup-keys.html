Matchup Keys

What stands out in the picked game, read from the League Stats Chart's numbers (ranked across all 30 teams). Every number shows the team's number and its rank, both shaded on the 8-step scale: green = good for that team (1st), red = 30th; on offense the most of a thing is green, on defense allowing the least is green. Hover a number for the exact figure. Edges (by team): one side in the top 10 and the other in the bottom half (a make rate only for an offense that takes those shots), or an offense that does something a lot against a defense that allows it the most (the offense's edge) or the least (the defense's edge). Clashes: the two teams at opposite ends of a number (Inside Vs Outside = one lives behind the arc, the other in the paint), a weak spot tonight's offense rarely goes at (Not Tested), or a team that takes lots of a shot with its makes 10+ places lower (Few Falling). Same Style: both teams at the same end of a number, or both teams' games leaning Over / Under (6+ decisions, 70%+). Strongest first in every group. Season / Last 10 / 5 / 3 use every game a team has until it has played that many. Shares, rates and FG% are percents; Trans Pts = points a 100 transition possessions.
