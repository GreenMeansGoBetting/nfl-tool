Matchup Keys

One game's strengths and weaknesses, read from the League Stats Chart's numbers (ranked across all 30 teams). Every tile is shaded on the 8-step scale: green = good for that team (1st), red = 30th; on defense allowing the least is green, so a bad defense is red. Hover any number for the exact figure. Overview: each team's Edges (its own column first; OFF = when it has the ball, DEF = when the other team does): one side in the top 10 and the other in the bottom half, or an offense that does something a lot against a defense that allows it the most (or a defense that allows it the least). The Matchup Summary reads one matchup a side: the road team's offense against the home team's defense, then the home team's offense against the road team's defense; a logo beside a category = the team with an Edge there. Both Teams: both at the same end of a number, or both teams' games leaning Over / Under (6+ decisions, 70%+). Categories: a line a category, both matchups, and a Read: who has the Edge, Even, or Not Tested (a weak defense tonight's offense rarely goes at). Click a line to open it. Rotations & History opens the Matchup Data page for the same game. Show All Summaries = every game's card that day, two a row. Season / Last 10 / 5 / 3 use every game a team has until it has played that many. Trans Pts = points a 100 transition possessions.
