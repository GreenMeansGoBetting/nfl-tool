Zone Matchup Targets

Where each player shoots from, and how (Self-Created, Catch & Shoot, Drives & Cuts), against what tonight's defense gives up to players like him (guards, forwards or bigs) from those same spots. Shots = how much more (or less) of that group's shooting this defense gives up from his spots than the league does: more chances. Makes = how much more (or less) those shots go in against this defense, from his zones and his shot types, in points a shot. Score = the two together: a defense that gives up lots of his shots but few makes lands near Toss Up, and you decide which matters. Over / Under past 5%, Strong past 12%. Every number is raw, this season only, nothing pulled toward the league: check the shot counts and judge the sample yourself. Not priced: the line is shown for reference only.
