Assist Spots

Every assists line tonight, read from both sides: who he passes to, how he sets them up, and who's out. Not picks: the reads point, you decide.

Assists = chances x his share: his teammates' made shots while he's on the floor, and how many of them he set up. Reads (each his side against tonight's): Targets = who he passes to against what tonight's defense allows their positions; Types = how he sets them up (kick-out 3s, drives, cuts and lobs, off-dribble 3s, short and mid) against what the defense allows of each; Lineup = a teammate out tonight who played 2 of the last 3, with his share without him against his season. The Read is Over or Under when they agree, Mixed when they pull both ways, No Read when nothing pulls enough. Under 20 assists this season is a Small Sample. Chances (pace and the baskets both teams make and allow) and Volume (lots of threes off a pass) are context, never the call. Defense ranks: 30th = allows the most (red). Go back a day and each Over / Under read shows Hit or Miss against his line then; YTD Model Performance has the season record. Click a row for Who He Assists, Assist Types, Game Log, The Read, Vs The Line and Lineup. NBA lineups change late: check the news before you bet.
