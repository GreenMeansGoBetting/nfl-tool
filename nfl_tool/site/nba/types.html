Player Types

Every player with 50+ shots this season, grouped by how he shoots, not his position: a PG can be an E, a center a G. A shoots from the deepest, K lives at the rim. Each number is the share of his own shots (Avg Dist in feet), shaded against every typed player on the 8-step scale: green = the most. Two letters (B / E) = borderline: nearly as close to the second type as his own; borderline players change letters often, so both are shown. Catch & Shoot = jumpers off a pass; Drive = driving and running finishes; Cut = cutting and plain layups / dunks (mostly assisted); I = bigs from G who take under 20% threes. The groups are redrawn every week; each keeps its letter. Heaves are left out. Source: ESPN play-by-play.
