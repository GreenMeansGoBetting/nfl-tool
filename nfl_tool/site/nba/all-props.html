All Props

Every points, threes, assists and rebounds line tonight on one list, sorted by how many of each player's reads point Over. No projections and no picks: real games from this season, so you can dig in and make the call.

What's here: a row for each player and stat, with the same numbers as that stat's own page. Stat says which line the row is for (click it for that stat's page). O / U count the Reads in his research panel that say Over / Under, out of every Read on that stat's page; the reads compare him to his position, so a star facing a weak defense leans Over, and the line already knows how good he is. Line is the middle of the books' lines; Over / Under are the best prices across your books (click one for every book, then click a price to save it to My Plays). Hit Rate counts his games over tonight's line. Opp Allows is what tonight's opponent gives up a game in that stat to his position (1st = the most). Click a row for that stat's research panel. NBA lineups change late: rest days and late scratches happen right up to tip, so check the news before you bet.
