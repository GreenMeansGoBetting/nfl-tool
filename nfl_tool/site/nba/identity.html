The Teams

Offense / Defense tags: up to four things each team does far more or less than the league (a full standard deviation or more), biggest first. 4th quarter: how often the team was up or down 15+ in the 4th, and how many of that night's starters were on the floor then on average (low = the coach empties the bench); "Within 5" is the same in close 4th quarters. By quarter: points scored minus allowed per 100 possessions in each quarter, colored against the league.

Offense vs Defense

Every row reads both sides: what the offense does next to what this defense lets its opponents do. Green = good for that side, colored against the league; hover a number for its league rank. Mismatch / Tough = the two sides are a full standard deviation apart one way or the other; Good vs Good = both clearly good. How OFTEN a team does something (pace, shot shares) isn't good or bad, so it isn't colored: Fits = the offense does it a lot and this defense gives it up a lot; Takes away = the offense does it a lot and this defense rarely allows it. Transition = a shot or turnover within 8 seconds of a defensive rebound or a steal.

Data: sportsdataverse / hoopR (ESPN play-by-play), rebuilt into possessions with the ten players on the floor.
