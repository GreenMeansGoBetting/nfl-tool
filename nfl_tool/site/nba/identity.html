Matchup Extremes

How each team does against one type of opponent compared with its own games against everyone, next to how the whole league moves against that type. Every number is that gap: +12 Pts = this team's games against these opponents ran 12 points a 100 possessions further from its normal than the league's do. A league average near zero can hide one team that falls apart and one that doesn't care; this finds them. Facing Offenses rows are this team's defense (what those opponents did to it, red = it struggles); Facing Defenses rows are its offense (green = it thrives). Pace is never good or bad, so it isn't colored. A type = the top or bottom third of the league in that number. Tonight = tonight's opponent is that type. Pts, 3PM, FTA and TOV are a 100 possessions. Small samples early in the season: check GP (games against that type), and hover a number for the details.

Schedule Spots

The same idea for the schedule: how each team does in a spot (back-to-backs, long road trips, altitude...) compared with its own games overall, next to how the whole league moves in that spot. Pts = its points a 100 possessions (green = it scores more), Opp Pts = what it allows (red = it allows more), pace isn't colored. Tonight = the team is in that spot in this game. GP = games in that spot this season: early on these are small samples. Rest Edge = 2+ more days off than the opponent; a road trip or home stand counts games in a row away or at home; National TV / NBA Cup = a national broadcast or a Cup game.

The Teams

Offense / Defense tags: up to four things each team does far more or less than the league (a full standard deviation or more), biggest first. 4th quarter: how often the team was up or down 15+ in the 4th, and how many of that night's starters were on the floor then on average (low = the coach empties the bench); "Within 5" is the same in close 4th quarters. By quarter: points scored minus allowed per 100 possessions in each quarter, colored against the league.

Offense vs Defense

Every row reads both sides: what the offense does next to what this defense lets its opponents do. Green = good for that side, colored against the league; hover a number for its league rank. Mismatch / Tough = the two sides are a full standard deviation apart one way or the other; Good vs Good = both clearly good. How OFTEN a team does something (pace, shot shares) isn't good or bad, so it isn't colored: Fits = the offense does it a lot and this defense gives it up a lot; Takes away = the offense does it a lot and this defense rarely allows it. Transition = a shot or turnover within 8 seconds of a defensive rebound or a steal.

Data: sportsdataverse / hoopR (ESPN play-by-play), rebuilt into possessions with the ten players on the floor.
