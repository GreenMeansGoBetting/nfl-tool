Play: the side of his main points line (SmartStake) our projection leans. Proj: the points we project (in testing). GMG Fair: our chance as a price with no cut. Odds: the best price across your books; click it for every book. Good To: the worst price that still leaves a 3-point edge. Edge: our chance minus the chance that price needs to break even, in points; green from 3 (a play), red below 0 (the price isn't good enough: not a play, not graded). Avg / FGA: points and shots a game this season. Hit Rate: how often he's gone over tonight's line in his last 5, last 10, the season at home and on the road ("x of y"), colored by the percentage. They count his own numbers over the line, whichever side the play is. Share: his share of his team's shots while he's on the floor, colored by where it ranks among this season's players. Mix: the % of his shots at the rim, from 3 and self-created, each colored by where it ranks among this season's players. Tags read both sides: Zone fit / Tough zones = this defense allows a high / low make rate where he shoots; Usage + = rotation teammates listed Out tonight (their shot share); Min ↑ = a rotation player at his spot is listed Out tonight; Min ↓ = one sat the last game and isn't listed Out tonight. Click any column to sort; pick a tag to see only those players. Click a player for with / without a teammate, every book's price and his shots against this defense.
