Opening Drive Tool

The GMG Discord has enjoyed playing a "First Drive - Punt" parlay to begin NFL Primetime games. This tool is designed to give data or how teams have performed at the start of games. Samples are small and this type of wager is for ENTERTAINMENT and RECREATION. This tool is more art than math.

Each team's first drive with the ball, graded the way the book grades it: TD (green), FG Att (yellow: made, missed or blocked), Punt (red: blocked punts too), and Other (blue) (turnover, turnover on downs, safety, a defensive or special-teams score). Offense = how that team's own first drive ended. Defense = how its opponents' first drives ended against it. 3 & Out = punted without a first down. Sample switches every number to the team's first-half drives or every drive of the game, for a bigger sample of the same thing. Fair = the team's offense and the other team's defense averaged (raw season numbers, no pull toward league average), as the price with no cut. Fair Parlay: pick an outcome for either team (or both) and it multiplies the chances (no correlation). Offense First / Defense First: the result in the weeks that side was on the field first, X when it wasn't. Plays and Yards are shaded against every opening drive in the league. Green = happens a lot for that outcome, red = rarely, yellow = about average.
