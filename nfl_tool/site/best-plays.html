Every player prop and TD bet this week where the GMG number and the best price across your books disagree, biggest edge first. GMG is the model's projection (props) or chance (TDs). It starts from the books' own line and moves toward the player's games, the matchup (what this defense allows to his position, both sides read) and the spread and total, only as far as the games played back it up. Fair is the price that chance is worth with no cut; Best price is the best across your books (click it for every book). Edge is the model's chance minus the chance the price needs to break even, in points. Rating: Strong and Lean by the size of the edge; Check = the model is far from every book, which usually means news it doesn't know (an injury, a role change), so look before you bet. ★ = the TD Summary's Key Player stars. Games that have kicked off drop off. Click a column to sort by it, a name for the player card, the box to save a play to My Plays.
