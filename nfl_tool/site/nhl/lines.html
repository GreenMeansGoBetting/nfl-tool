Line Matchups

Each forward line and defense pair, and how it has played together at 5-on-5 this season (three forwards, two defensemen and a goalie a side). Min is their 5-on-5 minutes together and the games they played together. Shot Share is their share of the shot attempts while they were on the ice (goals, shots on goal, misses and blocked shots): over 50% means they had the puck more than the other team. Goals is goals for and against with them on the ice. Last 3 GP is their shot share over their last three games together. Strong / Weak need 20 minutes together; Hot / Cold compare the last three games with their season. Likely Matchups: the home coach has the last change, so this shows which of his lines he has sent out most against each of the other team's lines in his home games this season (lines ranked by ice time that night). About a third of 5-on-5 time is played by mixed groups, so it is a lean, not a lock. Past Meetings: minutes trio against trio in these teams' earlier games this season. The lines are the NHL's projected lineup once it is posted (usually around midday ET); until then, each team's usual lines over its last five games.
