Where the Shots Come From

Shots per game from each part of the ice (on goal or wide; blocked shots and empty-net shots are left out). The big number is the offense's shots a game from there. The boxes are what the other defense allows from there: shots a game, that spot's share of all the shots it allows, and its league rank (said from the nearer end: "2nd most", "3rd fewest").
