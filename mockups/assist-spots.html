Assist Spots

Mockup: Real Numbers Through 11/4, Lines From Novig 11/5

An assist needs two things: he sets up the shot, and it goes in. His Role: Chances = teammates' made shots while he's on the floor (a 36); Share = how many of those he assisted. His Recipe: the shot type he sets up most, when it's clearly his style (No Clear Recipe otherwise). Tonight's Defense: pace, how much of what it allows comes off a pass, and how many attempts it allows of the shot types he sets up (1 = gives up the most). Who Else Handles: the teammate who changes his share the most, his share with that teammate on and off. Last Game: why his last number was what it was. Colors: the red-to-green rank scale. This season only, real counts, no projections.
